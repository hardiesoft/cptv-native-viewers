This installs support for CPTV thermal video files from The Cacophony Project:

  • VLC plugin: VLC plays .cptv files, and opens them when you double-click one in Finder.
  • Finder thumbnails and previews (macOS 13 or later): .cptv files show a thumbnail, and pressing Space plays a preview.

You'll need VLC 3.0 installed in your Applications folder. VLC itself isn't changed.
